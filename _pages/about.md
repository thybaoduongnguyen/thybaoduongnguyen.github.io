Hi, I'm Thy Duong. I am an M.Sc. candidate in Politics and Technology (Data & Society) at the Technical University of Munich, graduating in September 2026. My research covers AI governance, algorithmic accountability, and LLM auditing.

I study how model behavior shifts depending on who is asking, and what this means for citizens' trust in the democratic institutions and media systems that increasingly rely on AI-generated output. My work combines persona-conditioned experimental design, statistical testing, and text analysis.

Current research
======
In my master's thesis, supervised by Prof. Orestis Papakyriakopoulos, I audit geopolitical bias in LLM-based fact-checking. The study compares nine US, Chinese, and European models across five contested topics, using persona-conditioned prompting and close to 72,000 model responses. I presented this work as a poster at AI: Hype, Hope and Humanity (Erasmus University Rotterdam, September 2026), where it received the **Outstanding Poster Award**.
