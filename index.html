About

From December 2026 I am a PhD researcher at Leibniz University Hannover in the ERC-funded project BiASE-AI, led by Prof. Dr. Steffen Eckhard. My dissertation will examine bias in AI-mediated public service encounters and how it compares to bias in interactions with human civil servants.

My work combines experimental design, statistical testing and text analysis, and sits between computational social science, public administration and AI governance.

My interest in politics started early. In high school I debated competitively and represented Vietnam at the Harvard College World Schools Invitational.

Feel free to reach out by email.

Research

In my master's thesis, supervised by Prof. Orestis Papakyriakopoulos, I audited geopolitical bias in LLM-based fact-checking. The study compares nine US, Chinese and European models across five contested topics, using persona-conditioned prompting and close to 72,000 model responses.

I presented this work as a poster at the AI Hype, Hope and Humanity Festival at Erasmus University Rotterdam in September 2026, where it received the Outstanding Poster Award.
